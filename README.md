# Examify

Simple exam practice on your own computer. Take a short exam, review your answers
and pick up where you left off. Your questions and progress stay with you.

## Try it

Examify's Windows x64 and Linux x64 personal-study packages are in **preview**. There is
no published desktop release yet, and consumer OS/browser testing is still in
progress. The preview bundles its runtime; no development tools are needed.

1. [Get and open the preview](docs/solo-installation.md)
2. Choose **Try a sample exam** — five questions, no account or AI required
3. Review your result. Reopen **Examify** later to resume practice or see your progress

Ready for your own material? Choose **Create your question bank**, add notes or
PDFs, and review the generated questions before applying them. AI is optional
for sample practice. Generation and written-answer marking use your chosen
provider and may send it your material or answers.
[Read about privacy](docs/privacy.md).

[Back up, update or remove Examify](docs/solo-maintenance.md) ·

## Share with a household

For multiple people or access from another device, use the

accounts, invitations and normal sign-in.
