Cell biology: original study notes for a synthetic Examify demonstration

Chloroplasts in plant cells capture light energy and use it to make sugar.
Mitochondria release energy from food. Both plant and animal cells have mitochondria.
Plant cells have a cell wall that supports the cell. Animal cells do not have a cell wall.
The cell membrane controls what enters and leaves a cell.
The nucleus contains genetic information and helps control the cell's activities.
